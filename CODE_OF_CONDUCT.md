# Code of conduct

agentstat welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels,
and viewpoints. Everyone should be able to ask questions, report problems, and
participate without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent when asking for clarification.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and explain disagreements.
- Make room for newcomers and different approaches to solving a problem.
- Respect privacy, including credentials, account data, and private conversations.
- Accept corrections and follow reasonable requests from project maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone's private information without
permission are not acceptable. Repeated disruption, spam, and retaliation against
someone who reports a concern are also not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents the
project elsewhere. Technical disagreement is welcome when expressed respectfully.

## Reporting a concern

For a private report, use a contact method the repository owner makes available
on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/dhanushk-offl). If none is available,
open a minimal issue titled **Private conduct contact requested** without names,
incident details, or personal information, so a maintainer can arrange a private
channel. You do not need to disclose the concern publicly.

GitHub's **Report abuse** option on the relevant profile or content is another
route for behavior that violates GitHub's rules, particularly if the concern
involves the repository owner. For an immediate threat to someone's safety,
contact the appropriate local emergency service.

When a private channel is available, describe what happened, where it happened,
and any relevant links. Share only information needed to assess the report.
Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy and limit disclosure to what
is needed to address the concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a change in behavior, close a
disruptive discussion, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
The response should reflect the severity, context, and repetition of the behavior.

Anyone involved in a report should not decide its outcome when another
uninvolved maintainer is available. Requests to reconsider a decision can be made
through the same private channel, with relevant new information. Reports and
appeals must not be used to harass participants.
